# Zypher Marketplace — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

At **Zypher**, we prioritize your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and global best privacy practices.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect information necessary to facilitate seamless marketplace transactions:
* **Account Information**: Your full name, email address, contact telephone number, and encrypted password.
* **Delivery Information**: Shipping addresses, recipient contact details, and location landmarks.
* **Transaction Details**: Order history, purchased items, receipts, and payment method identifiers (note: sensitive credit card information is securely handled by PCI-DSS certified payment processors and never stored directly on our servers).
* **Communication Data**: Customer inquiries and chat logs between buyers and sellers within the platform.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
Your information is strictly utilized to:
* Process, fulfill, and track your marketplace orders.
* Connect you directly with sellers and logistics courier partners for successful deliveries.
* Prevent fraud, secure authenticated sessions, and protect account integrity.
* Provide customer support and address dispute resolutions.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
Zypher does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:
* **Logistics & Couriers**: Name, address, and contact number necessary for package delivery.
* **Merchant Sellers**: Relevant order specifications required to prepare and pack your order.
* **Legal Compliance**: Where mandated by law or court order to prevent fraudulent or illegal activities.

---

### 4. Data Security & Storage
We employ modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS), hashed password storage, and rigorous access controls to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of your personal data.

---

### 5. Your Rights as a Data Subject
You have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal account information at any time through your **Account Settings** or by contacting Zypher Data Protection at `privacy@zypher.ph`.
